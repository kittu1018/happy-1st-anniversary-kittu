My Love Letter to You 💌

Hey meri jaan,

Happy 1st Anniversary ❤️

Tumhe pata hai na, main aksar sochta hoon, agar tum meri life mein na aayi hoti, toh main kitna adhura hota. Tum meri zindagi ka woh part ho jo mujhe complete karta hai. Aur sach kahun toh, tumse baat karte hi lagta hai jaise duniya ruk gayi ho aur bas main aur tum reh gaye ho.

Mujhe tumhari wo chhoti chhoti baatein pagal kar deti hain,tumhara bina wajah hansi lena, apni baaton ko baar-baar repeat karna, ya phir kabhi bina soche seedha dil ki baat bol dena. Yehi chhoti chhoti cheezein mujhe tumse aur deeply pyaar karne lagti hain.

Ek saal mein tumne mujhe bas ek partner hi nahi, ek best friend, ek comfort zone aur ek safe place diya hai. Tumhare saath mujhe apni zindagi ki har problem halki lagti hai, aur har khushi double.

Main tumse ek hi promise karna chahta hoon, chahe din kitna bhi mushkil ho, chahe tum kitni bhi uljhan mein ho, main tumhara haath kabhi nahi chhodunga. Tum meri duniya ho, aur meri duniya hamesha tumhare saath hi complete hogi.

Future mein main tumhe apni har kahani ka hero/heroine banana chahta hoon, chaahe wo random trips ho, apna chhota sa ghar ho, ya raat ke 2 baje ki Maggie. Bas tum, main, aur humari apni duniya.

Ek saal sirf start hai meri jaan… main tumhe hamesha apni forever bana kar rakhna chahta hoon.

Forever tumhara,
Suar ❤️
